# ubeChores Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 30, 2026

ubeChores is published by ube media (Jakob Kubicki). The short version: we
keep what you and your house put into the app, use it only to run the app,
and never sell it or use it for advertising. There are no ads, analytics or
trackers in ubeChores.

## What we store

**Your account.** How you sign in decides what we receive:

- Sign in with Apple: an account identifier, your email address (or an
  Apple relay address if you choose Hide My Email), and your name if you
  share it.
- Google: an account identifier, your email address and your name.
- Email and password: your email address. Your password is stored only as
  a one-way hash by our sign-in provider; nobody can read it, including us.

If you use Sign in with Apple, we also keep a token from Apple so that
deleting your account can revoke the app's access to your Apple ID.

**Your profile.** A display name, an emoji, a colour theme, and either a
generated cartoon face or a photo you upload.

**What your house creates.** The house's name, its chores and schedules,
completed chores and any proof photos, emoji reactions, comments, nudges,
and plans you share with the house (such as a trip, with its dates and a
note).

**Invitations.** When you invite someone, we store the email address or
phone number you invited and whether they accepted. An invitation stops
working after 7 days. Email invitations are sent by us. Phone invitations are sent as a
text from your own Messages app. If you pick a number from your contacts,
iOS gives the app only that one number; the app can't read your contacts.

**Reports and blocks.** If you report a post or comment, we keep who
reported it, the reason, and a copy of what it said, and we email that to
our moderation inbox. If you block a housemate, we keep that you blocked
them. Blocks are private: the person you block isn't told.

**Your device.** A push notification token, so notifications from your
house reach your phone.

## What we don't collect

No location, no contacts list, no browsing or usage analytics, no
advertising identifiers, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

## Who can see it

Everything your house creates is visible only to the members of that house:
people who accepted a personal invitation. Your profile is visible to the
people in your houses. Moderation reports are seen only by us.

Apple may share aggregate statistics with us, such as download counts and
crash reports, through App Store Connect. That's governed by
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and your
device's "Share With App Developers" setting.

## Services we use

We use a small number of providers to run the app. Each gets only what it
needs to do its job:

- **Supabase** hosts the database, sign-in and photo storage. Production
  data is stored in Canada (Amazon Web Services, ca-central-1).
- **Brevo** delivers our emails: sign-in codes, invitations, house notices
  and moderation reports.
- **Apple** delivers push notifications and provides Sign in with Apple.
- **Google** provides Sign in with Google, if you use it.
- **blobatar.dev** draws the generated cartoon faces. It receives a random
  identifier for each face, never your name or email, and, like any website,
  the IP address the request comes from.

## Photos and the camera

The app uses the camera or your photo library only when you choose to add a
proof photo or a profile photo. Photos are stored privately and shown only
to the people in your house. A proof photo can be removed by whoever posted
it or by the house's homelord.

## Notifications

Reminders for your own chores are scheduled on your phone. Nudges, a
housemate's completed chores, overdue chores and shared plans arrive as push
notifications through Apple. You can turn notifications off in Settings at
any time.

## Deleting your data

- **Something you posted.** Delete a post, photo or comment in the app, and
  it's gone for the whole house.
- **Leaving a house.** Your future activity stops, and what you already
  posted stays with the house.
- **Your whole account.** Open the You tab and tap Delete account. Your
  account, profile, profile photo, house memberships, reactions, nudges,
  plans and push tokens are deleted straight away, and Sign in with Apple
  access is revoked. Chores you completed, their proof photos, and your
  comments stay in your house's history so it still adds up, but they're no
  longer linked to you: they show as "Someone".

We keep records of invitations, of the emails we've sent (the address and
the kind of email), and of moderation reports, so we can fix delivery
problems and deal with abuse. Email us and we'll delete any of this, or send you a copy of
your data.

## Children

ubeChores isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly
collect their information.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new
effective date. For a significant change, we'll also say so in the app's
release notes.
