# ubeChores Support

Questions, bugs or feedback? Email **jakobkub@gmail.com**. We reply within a
day, and usually much sooner.

## Something in your feed shouldn't be there

- **Report it.** Open the post's menu (the ••• button), or press and hold a
  comment, and choose Report. It disappears for you straight away, and we
  look at it within 24 hours.
- **Block someone.** From their post, their comment, or their row in the
  House tab, choose Block. You won't see their posts, comments or nudges,
  and they won't be told. Unblock them from the same row.
- **Remove it.** You can delete your own posts and any comment. The house's
  homelord can delete any post.

## Deleting your account

Open the You tab and tap Delete account. It happens straight away. What's
removed and what stays with your house is explained in the
[privacy policy](./#deleting-your-data).
